# Tidefall Round 9 implementation and verification

Implemented in the existing game, retaining the shared terrain, Rapier world, numerical water, authoritative combat and WebSocket room pipeline. This is a substantial modeled-world expansion, with the limitations below. It is not a photorealistic reconstruction or a claim that life, surface geology or oceans have been observed on either exoplanet.

Evidence: [final-code fixed-tick measurements](ROUND9-evidence.json), [real-time four-socket soak samples](ROUND9-soak.json), [regressions](tests/round9.test.ts), [network regressions](tests/round9-network.test.ts). 1. **Geological identification failures.** The old inspector inferred broad material from position rather than the actual detached object's identity. Instance hits lost their unit index, child metadata could swallow parent metadata, merged flora lacked a reliable triangle-to-organism mapping, and a physics hit could fall back to terrain when the visual ray missed. Rock placements and static colliders now retain registry identities. The inspector resolves instance IDs, merged face ranges, child metadata and the prediction world's collider identity. Ground classification follows the actual rendered terrain triangle, including barycentric contact properties. Tests cover twelve real detached-rock collider hits in both worlds and six actual THREE instance ray hits. Browser checks identified columnar basalt LT-033 and carbonate mudstone LT-043 as distinct geological targets.

2. **Terrain and microgeometry.** Added small flow- and fault-aligned joint relief to the existing 129 by 129 terrain mesh, six coherent parent outcrop groups, polygonal cooling columns and irregular flattened bed ledges. Marine seed 42 has 63 structural outcrop pieces; volcanic groups use columns. Strike guides detached-rock orientation. Registry-driven world-space grain, bedding, joints, clasts, crusts and veins supplement physical relief. Existing loose-cover relaxation and shoreline triangle clipping remain integrated. The terrain grid is still 2.5 m; small shader relief does not create separate collision cavities. There is no adaptive mesh or geological-timescale erosion solver.

3. **Lithologies, facies and composition.** The central catalog contains 32 modeled units: sixteen lithological units, sediment deposits, mineral accumulations and alteration units. Examples include vesicular basalt, columnar basalt, dolerite, tuff, carbonate mudstone, grainstone, chert, volcaniclastic sandstone, beach sand and mineral crusts. Seven facies modify physical and visual properties; six surface treatments drive microstructure. Units provide mineral-assemblage ranges, texture, origin, history, density, friction, porosity, strength, permeability, solubility, conductivity, resistance and repose. Seed 42 actually uses 25 units and 98 unit-facies combinations; seed -43 uses 15 units and 61 combinations. These are not 98 independent rock species, measured exoplanet properties or a full thermodynamic chemistry solver.

4. **Biological forms and honest counts.** Ten sessile functional forms: mat, crust, tuft, frond, branch, fan, tube, float, filament and rosette. Eight parameter recipes per form produce **76 distinct mesh hashes**, rather than eighty unique geometries. Seed 42 instantiates **260 stationary organisms and 71 morphology designations**, distributed among coast, upland, cavern and hydrothermal-fringe associations. Representative 20 m-radius regions contain 14, 4, 2, 18 and 2 stationary organisms; exact coordinates are in the evidence JSON. Six marine mobile classes comprise hexapodal stalker, armored ridgeback, four-limbed ambusher, segmented six-limbed grazer, eight-limbed scuttler and limbless aquatic skimmer. There are three hostile classes and three passive or defensive classes; the initial marine population is seven agents, including four hostiles. Twelve mobile parameter variants per class are available, not seventy-two simultaneously instantiated species. Lineage and ecology are authored speculative grammars. Pyrax has zero native stationary organisms and retains two machine enemy classes.

5. **Creature threat and encounters.** Predators gain 44 m vision, 6.8 m/s pursuit, 32-point melee hits and a 1.45-second attack cooldown, with telegraphs and server validation retained. Ambushers pounce and charge; grazers flee; injured scuttlers defend themselves; shallow-water skimmers filter and wander. Segmented anatomy and limb counts drive distinct silhouettes and gait. Habitat/productivity influences encounter staging. The director caps active population at twelve and storage at twenty-four reusable slots. Loot is bounded and expires. A regression found that feeding collision-resolved displacement back into AI steering caused downhill drift; steering now stays independent, while animation reads separate motion speed. Passive grace and early survival tests pass.

6. **Atmosphere and clouds.** Replaced the obvious sinusoidal cloud pattern with wind-advected three-dimensional value noise, domain warp, organized banks, mass and fine detail, sun-facing gradients and weather-dependent opacity. The final shader uses 26 noise samples per fragment. Condensation is suppressed in the dry volcanic profile; ash is separate. Repeated storm envelopes and the late cold/radiant front span normal survival. These remain atmospheric layers, not volumetric fluid clouds with resolved cloud shadows. The storm screenshot shows the implemented bank structure.

7. **Water and tsunami.** Preserved one authoritative 65 by 65 local-inertial water field, shared wave-contact reconstruction, wetting/drying and triangle-clipped shoreline. Duration is now propagated through weather, prediction, survival, rendering and water contact. The normal warning occurs at 795 s and source release at 810 s; preview uses 75 s and 90 s. The benchmark compares disturbed water against an undisturbed baseline: stations at z=20, 0 and -10 cross the additional-depth/current criterion 16.1, 17.8 and 18.8 seconds after warning, with maximum additional depths 1.657, 1.274 and 1.126 m. The z=35 station lies within the initial source lobe and is not evidence of propagation. Final mass residual is about 1.2e-8 cubic metres for that experiment. This remains reduced shallow-water physics, without breaking-wave spray or sealed-room air hydraulics.

8. **Thirty-minute pacing and stability.** Default rooms last 1,800 s. Rescue announces at 24:00, approaches at 26:30, lands at 27:00, closes at 29:00, launches at 29:10, reaches the orbital presentation at 29:45 and finishes at 30:00. Preview lasts 240 s: approach 3:00, landing 3:10, close 3:40, launch 3:45, orbit 3:55, finish 4:00. Room creation controls mode; joins and rematches retain it. Both schedules preserve ordered stages and real physics steps. A real-time server run completed **1,800.99 wall seconds**, **108,001 ticks**, and **16,544 snapshots on each of four real sockets**. Four scripted insulated players waited inside sealed rooms; two boarded by scripted socket requests and two missed departure. Both extracted players tied first. Post-GC heap went from about 51.93 to 47.75 MB, remaining around 47–48 MB after warmup; body and AI counts were bounded. That soak used an earlier Round 9 core before the final gangway, collider-identity and presentation refinements. It is not a 30-minute native rendered playthrough. A separate final-code fixed-tick run also completed all 108,001 ticks. Preview scheduling and end-to-end extraction are covered by executed simulation/network regressions; the browser ascent recording uses a staged normal room.

9. **Facility and interactions.** Tidefall has ten rooms: geology laboratory, biology containment, atmospheric analysis, operations, equipment store, medical station, crew quarters, power and maintenance, observation gallery and emergency refuge. They connect to a central corridor and two-door entrance airlock. There are 276 static parts, eleven glazing volumes and fourteen synchronized doors including two legacy shelter doors. Equipment includes bunks, consoles, cylinders, racks, power banks and specimen furniture. E reads room records and operates doors. Airlock interlocks close the other clear door first; obstruction checks prevent closing on a capsule. Room bounds and the actual closed door govern environmental protection. Portable geological samples can be carried. Browser testing entered the airlock, walked the corridor, read a biology record and viewed exterior scenery through glazing. Physics tests trace every room doorway and solid observation glass in server and prediction worlds. Enemies use those same colliders. Destructible glass, working laboratory chemistry, medical machinery and room pressure exchange are incomplete. Pyrax intentionally has no marine research facility.

10. **Spacecraft and extraction.** The rescue now has a glazed cabin, ribs, consoles, four restrained seats, engine pods, gear, a retracting hatch and one continuous inclined gangway. E boarding requires entering the cabin threshold. Boarding registers an assigned passenger seat; free cabin walking ends then. Ship parts, ramp and hatch use shared rendering/collision recipes. Landed hull bodies become fixed during boarding and return to kinematic bodies for departure: repeated kinematic writes had prevented a walking capsule from climbing reliably. A new regression walks 300 ticks into the cabin without jumping and boards successfully. The same walk and E action succeeded in the browser. Quintic motion blends landing, departure, pitch and ascent; compressed physical altitude supports a modeled 320 km orbital presentation. Atmospheric extinction falls away, stars appear, and a curved reconstructed planet is visible through windows. A continuous 49-second staged window-ascent recording and final result screenshot were captured.

11. **Takeoff stutter diagnosis and fix.** The ship mesh previously extrapolated presentation time while the seated camera followed raw 10 Hz snapshot time. Two different clocks produced relative jitter. Connection clock offset now smooths, CameraRig samples a single frame time, and ship and passenger camera share it. The camera uses the same pitched local-to-world passenger transform as the hull, without competing transform writers. Boarding freezes capsule locomotion and actions. Finite-difference continuity and transform tests pass. The browser recording shows the final presentation; it is not instrumented native GPU proof that every hardware configuration is stutter-free.

12. **Star map and celestial rendering.** The map now uses bundled measured neighboring orbital distances for TRAPPIST-1 e/f and 55 Cancri e/b, explicitly labeled logarithmic display scale and illustrative phase. Scenario globes use survey-derived terrain shading and respect liquid inventory, so Pyrax does not acquire a blue ocean. Retained HYG star data and planetary sky transforms remain authoritative. Orbit uses a bounded cinematic sphere, procedural land/ocean/cloud reconstruction, atmospheric rim and the local stellar direction. The current orbital screenshot has a dark curved limb and faint stars through the narrow cabin window; it is not a high-detail globe or precision orbital ephemeris. Celestial inspection through the ship glass remains limited.

13. **Physical interactions.** Unit density, friction and resistance feed existing physical placement and interaction behavior. Three portable samples have real low-mass bodies and exclusive server carry ownership. G or Tab carries/releases them; line of sight, range, collision, death and disconnect govern release. Heavy boulders remain push/field targets. Snapshot rotations now interpolate, making rolling objects visibly rotate. Doors, window volumes, irregular outcrop hulls and rescue ramp are shared. Existing rifle impulses, aiming, loot exclusivity and field mechanics remain covered by regression tests. Detailed fracture and chemical dissolution are not implemented.

14. **Scientific inspector.** Compact dwell readouts show the actual unit or organism designation, with composition, history and modeled physical properties in Tab discoveries. Contacts retain mixed triangle properties and contributing unit IDs. Biological records distinguish functional form, parameter variant, habitat and speculative lineage. Facility stations explain which environmental claims are modeled. Rescue self-inspection is hidden aboard so it does not obscure windows. The nearby-surface suit lamp now reduces power smoothly to improve material readability. A brief old label can remain during the dwell interval immediately after looking away.

The separate four-socket real-time soak verifies continuing snapshot delivery, clock progression, extraction and scoring. 19. **Remaining defects and scope limits.** Visuals remain stylized, including simple creature surfaces and box-derived interior equipment. The orbital limb is dark and the window composition is restrictive; no high-resolution whole-planet model was added. Clouds are layered shaders, not resolved volumetric weather. Small-scale terrain relief is partly shader detail. Biology is a deterministic grammar, not evolution or hundreds of simulated species. Station records, doors and carrying work; medical machines, chemistry, breakable windows and room gas hydraulics do not. Native Pointer Lock, unrestricted turning and Escape/Resume were not reverified this round because the embedded browser blocks capture; the earlier user's Edge confirmation is historical evidence only. Touch devices, Internet multiplayer, native long-session rendering, public deployment and Docker execution remain unverified. Large client bundles, storm rendering and snapshot bandwidth remain practical limits.

## Scientific provenance

All new unit assemblages and their property ranges are representative simulation inputs, not measured samples. Geological treatments were informed by [USGS cooling-joint descriptions](https://www.usgs.gov/observatories/hvo/news/volcano-watch-columnar-jointing-provides-clues-cooling-history-lava-flows) and [USGS igneous/metamorphic aquifer context](https://www.usgs.gov/mission-areas/water-resources/science/igneous-and-metamorphic-rock-aquifers). Retained astronomical data and licenses remain in `src/data/README.md`; the [NASA archive parameter definitions](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/API_PS_columns.html) and [TAP documentation](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/TAP/usingTAP.html) distinguish catalog constraints from scenario assumptions. No observed exoplanet biosphere, ocean, moon or surface map is asserted.
